Our Story

Built around the first flame.

Aurelia started with a simple belief: that the best flavor still comes from fire. Every plate that leaves our kitchen passes over live coals or through the hearth — not for show, but because it's simply how good food is made.

We keep the menu close to the season, source from growers we know by name, and finish every detail — bread, butter, bitters — by hand. The room is warm on purpose. So is the welcome.
